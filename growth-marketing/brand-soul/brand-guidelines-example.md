# {Product} Brand Guidelines - Example

A condensed, debranded version of a real brand book for an AI money assistant. Use it as a structure. Colours, fonts and logos are placeholders.

The soul document ([template](soul-template.md)) defines who {Assistant} is *inside* the relationship. These guidelines cover how the brand looks and sounds *before* that relationship exists: ads, app store pages, onboarding, the first day.

---

## 1. The big idea

One line every decision traces back to:

> **Finally, a money person on your side.**

The story behind it: having someone truly in your corner with money used to depend on who you knew. A private advisor, a friend in finance. Everyone else made do with headlines and guesswork. {Product} closes that gap - not with more charts, but with someone who pays attention so you don't have to.

**The test:** Does the first person who tries {Assistant} feel like they finally have someone on their side?

---

## 2. Goals

| Type | Goal |
|---|---|
| Perception | Own the "money friend" category. Signal intelligence without explaining it. |
| Relationship | Feel indispensable, not just useful. Replace worry with confidence. |
| Impact | Users can point to one moment where {Assistant} showed them something they'd never have found alone. |

---

## 3. Audience

Describe each group in a line. Mark which ones the brand leads with.

| Group | Profile | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Casual | Invests now and then, overwhelmed, short on time | Awareness |
| Curious | Growing interest, needs an easy way in | Awareness |
| Informed | Knows the basics, follows finance content | Primary |
| Engaged | Trades actively, frustrated by slow information | Primary |
| Sophisticated | Data-driven, values signal over noise | Secondary |
| Expert | Professional level, very high bar | Secondary |

**One promise per group, one thing that never changes:** {Assistant} is not a tool. It's the money friend most people never had.

### Anti-personas: who we do *not* want to attract

| Anti-persona | What pulls them in | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Pseudo-expert | Jargon, "look how smart this is" language | Showing off data volume or technical terms |
| Gambler | Tips, urgency, fear of missing out | Anything that sounds like a hot tip or "before it's too late" |
| Chaos fan | Self-mockery, chaotic energy | Lower risk; monitor |

---

## 4. Personality

Each trait has a **shadow** - what it must never turn into.

| Trait | What it means | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Quietly confident | Shows intelligence instead of announcing it | Arrogant or condescending |
| Genuinely personal | Feels made for you, not a segment | Creepy or intrusive |
| Honest without apology | Says what's true, not what's comfortable | Cold or clinical |
| Calm under pressure | The still voice when markets are loud. No invented urgency. | Distant or indifferent |
| Effortlessly intelligent | Turns complexity into clarity before it reaches you | Showing off |

**In one sentence:** the brilliant friend who already knows - calm enough to never panic, honest enough to never flatter, personal enough to never feel generic.

---

## 5. Brand layers

If you have a company and a character, decide which one speaks where.

| Context | Who leads |
|---|---|
| In-app, social, consumer | {Assistant} |
| Paid ads, SEO | {Assistant}, with {Product} visible |
| Press, partnerships, fundraising | {Product} (the company) |

**When in doubt:** Is this about the relationship or the company? Relationship -> {Assistant}'s voice. Company -> {Product}'s voice.

---

## 6. Voice

### {Assistant} before the relationship (marketing, onboarding)

| Principle | Not this | But this |
|---|---|---|
| Lead with the outcome, not the feature | "Scans hundreds of thousands of data points daily." | "The thing that would have changed your decision this morning - it had it ready." |
| Speak to one person | "For investors who want an edge." | "For the morning you open the app and everything you need is already there." |
| Give before you ask | A call to action in the first line | A sharp observation first. Ask later. |

### {Product} as a company (press, partners)

| Principle | Not this | But this |
|---|---|---|
| Smart without performing | "Powered by dozens of data feeds and a multi-layer engine." | "The right signal, for the right person, at the right moment." |
| Conviction without attacking others | "Unlike other apps that overwhelm you..." | "Most market tools were built for the market. This one was built for you." |
| Fair without preaching | "For too long, the wealthy..." | "The kind of help that used to need a private advisor. Now in your pocket." |
| Forward without overpromising | "It will transform your financial life." | "It gets smarter about you every day." |

---

## 7. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Show the facts and let the user decide | Say or imply buy, sell or hold |
| Let results speak | Brag ("we spotted it first", "we were right") |
| Point to what changed for *you* | Repeat news everyone already saw |
| Say "I don't have that data" | Fill gaps with "the market thinks..." |
| Advertise only what is live today | Promise features that aren't built |
| Use numbers you can verify | Use performance claims nobody can check |
| Stay quiet when nothing matters | Send "daily tips" to hit engagement targets |

---

## 8. Words and phrases to avoid

- **Fear and guilt:** "Don't miss out", "Before it's too late", "Did you miss it?"
- **Fake urgency:** "Act now", "Limited time", countdown timers
- **Tip-service:** "moonshot", "huge gains", "we tell you what to buy"
- **Predictions as our call:** "{Assistant} predicts...", "{Assistant}'s price target"
- **Self-description:** "As your AI financial assistant..."
- **Machinery:** internal system names, engine names, "AI is analysing your preferences"
- **Inflated numbers:** any coverage or performance number not checked against the live product

## 10. Review before launch

Every creative is checked against these guidelines and the soul's "never" list. Soul or truth violations are a **kill**. Tone, format and visual issues are a **revise**. See the [README](README.md) for how the review works.
