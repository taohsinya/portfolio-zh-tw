# Voice Modifier: Healthcare > Medical Devices

## Uses Model C (Industry)

## Required Data
- Company + product name
- Regulatory status (FDA 510(k), PMA, De Novo, CE Mark)
- Key performance spec
- Revenue or market share
- Competitive comparison
- Adoption metric (procedure volume, install base)

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Medical device stories need clinical context:
- "Shield test detects colorectal cancer from a blood draw (sensitivity: 83.1%, specificity: 89.6%)"
- Explain what the device does BEFORE getting into financials.

## Regulatory Pathway Note
- 510(k): cleared as substantially equivalent to an existing device (lower bar)
- PMA: approved based on clinical data (higher bar, stronger moat)
- De Novo: new device type, no predicate
