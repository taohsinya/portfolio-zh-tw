# Voice Modifier: Finance > Banking

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Bank name + ticker
- NII/NIM (net interest income/margin)
- Provision for credit losses
- Loan growth rate
- CET1 ratio
- ROE
- Stock reaction (if earnings)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain NIM for broader audience: "NIM of 2.85% means the bank earns $2.85 for every $100 in interest-earning assets."

## Rate Sensitivity Context
Always connect bank earnings to the rate environment. "Each 25bp Fed cut reduces JPM's NII by ~$600M annually."
