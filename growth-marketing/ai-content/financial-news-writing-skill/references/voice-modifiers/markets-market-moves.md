# Voice Modifier: Markets > Market Moves

## Uses Model E (Equity Market), NOT Model D

## Required Context Layers
1. **Headline level**: S&P 500, Dow, Nasdaq Composite — exact level + % change
2. **Breadth**: Advance/decline ratio, new 52-week highs vs lows
3. **Sector rotation**: All 11 GICS sectors ranked by % move
4. **Volatility**: VIX level + direction + percentile vs trailing 1 year
5. **Cross-asset**: 10Y yield direction, DXY, gold, oil — pick most relevant 2

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Reader knows what the S&P is. But explain WHY it moved — connect data release/event to market reaction. Show the transmission mechanism.

## Banned Phrases (Market Moves specific)
- "amid uncertainty" → name the specific uncertainty
- "market sentiment" → name the data point that shifted positioning
- "risk-on" / "risk-off" → only if followed by VIX or flow data
- "mixed session" → specify which sectors up, which down, by how much
