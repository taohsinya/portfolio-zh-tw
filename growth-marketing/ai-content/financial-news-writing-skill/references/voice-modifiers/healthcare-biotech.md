# Voice Modifier: Healthcare > Biotech

## Uses Model C (Industry)

## Required Data
- Company + ticker
- Technology platform (CAR-T, gene therapy, mRNA, CRISPR, etc.)
- Lead candidate + indication
- Clinical data (efficacy + significance)
- Pipeline breadth (programs by phase)
- Cash runway
- Partnership/licensing deal if event

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Biotech requires the most explanation of any sub-tag. Define:
- The technology platform (one sentence)
- The mechanism of action (one sentence)
- Why this approach is different from standard of care

## Tone Calibration
- Science is the story. Let data drive the narrative.
- Always include cash runway — biotech burns cash. "X has $Y in cash, providing runway through [date]."
