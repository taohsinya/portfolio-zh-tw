# Voice Modifier: Tech & AI > AI & ML

## Key Terminology
Model name, parameter count, benchmark scores (MMLU, HumanEval), training cost,
inference cost per token, context window, modality support.

## Required Comparisons
ALWAYS compare to: the predecessor model AND the closest competitor.
"Claude 4 scores 92% on MMLU vs Claude 3.5's 88% and GPT-5's 90%."

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
This is the sub-tag most prone to AI hype. Counter with precision:
- ❌ "AI model shows remarkable capabilities"
- ✅ "Claude 4 scores 92.1% on MMLU, a 4.1-point improvement over Claude 3.5, and reduces inference cost to $0.003 per 1K tokens from $0.008"

## Revenue Connection (mandatory)
Every AI story must tie to dollars: training cost, inference revenue, CapEx.
"The $4B training run required 25,000 H100 GPUs over 90 days."
