# Voice Modifier: Economy > Trade & Tariffs

## Required Data Points
- Countries involved
- Specific tariff rate(s)
- Products/sectors affected
- Dollar value of affected trade
- Effective date
- Retaliatory measures (if any)

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Trade stories affect broad audiences. Explain supply chain implications in plain language.
"A 25% tariff on Chinese EVs adds ~$12,000 to the sticker price of a $48,000 vehicle."

## Tone Calibration
- Geopolitical framing is acceptable but must be backed by trade data.
- Always quantify: "affects $X billion in bilateral trade."
