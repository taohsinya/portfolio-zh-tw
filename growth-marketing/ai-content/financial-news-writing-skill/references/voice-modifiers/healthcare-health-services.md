# Voice Modifier: Healthcare > Health Services

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Company or insurer name
- Revenue or premium figure
- Medical loss ratio (MLR) or cost metric
- Membership or patient count
- Policy change affecting sector
- Stock/sector performance

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain industry-specific metrics:
- "Medical loss ratio of 85% means the insurer paid $0.85 of every premium dollar on medical claims."
- "Risk adjustment: CMS pays insurers more for sicker patients; inflating diagnoses increases payments."

## Policy Context
Medicare Advantage, ACA marketplace, Medicaid redetermination — always specify which program.
