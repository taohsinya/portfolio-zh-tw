# Voice Modifier: Tech & AI > Hardware & Devices

## Key Terminology
ASP (average selling price), BOM (bill of materials), units shipped, attach rate.

## Required Data
- Product name + category
- Key spec vs prior generation
- Price point
- Availability date
- Target market + expected volume

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Consumer hardware stories need consumer-friendly language. Translate specs:
"The M4 Ultra contains 80B transistors — 4x the M2 Ultra — enabling real-time 8K video rendering."

## Tone Calibration
- For robotics/emerging: higher explanation depth. Define form factors, DOF, use cases.
- For smartphones/laptops: lower depth. Focus on price, availability, competitive positioning.
