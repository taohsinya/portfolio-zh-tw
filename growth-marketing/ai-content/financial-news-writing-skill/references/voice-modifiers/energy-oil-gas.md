# Voice Modifier: Energy > Oil & Gas

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Benchmark: WTI or Brent (always specify which)
- Price per barrel + daily/weekly change ($ and %)
- Supply factor: OPEC production, US rig count, inventory (EIA)
- Demand factor: seasonal, economic indicator
- Geopolitical factor (if relevant)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Commodity traders know the market. Focus on: what changed today and why.

## Benchmark Precision
Always name the benchmark. "Oil" alone is banned. Use "WTI crude" or "Brent crude."
