# Voice Modifier: Economy > Economic Data

## Required Data Points
- Release name (CPI, NFP, GDP, PMI, etc.)
- Actual value + consensus + prior
- Headline vs core (for CPI/PCE/PPI)
- 1-2 key component breakdowns
- Market reaction: at least 1 asset move

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain what the data means for policy, not just what the number is.
"Core CPI at 3.1% means [X] for the Fed because [Y]."

## Tone Calibration
- Lead with the surprise direction (hotter/cooler than expected), not just the number.
- Always contextualize: where in the cycle? Trend direction over 3-6 months.
