# Voice Modifier: Finance > Real Estate

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Property type (office, multifamily, industrial, retail)
- Transaction price or valuation
- Cap rate or yield
- Occupancy rate
- Rent growth metric
- Geographic market

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain cap rate: "A 5.2% cap rate means the property generates $5.20 in net income for every $100 of value."

## For REITs
Use FFO/AFFO (not EPS). "AFFO of $1.42/share" is the correct earnings metric.
