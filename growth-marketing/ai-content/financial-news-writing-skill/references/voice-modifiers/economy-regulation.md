# Voice Modifier: Economy > Regulation

## Required Data Points
- Regulatory body name
- Specific rule/action
- Companies/sectors affected
- Effective date or timeline
- Financial impact estimate

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain what the regulation does in plain language. Quote the specific rule or statute.
"Section 45V of the IRA provides a $3/kg tax credit for green hydrogen."

## Tone Calibration
- Neutral on policy merits. Report the rule, the affected parties, and market reaction.
- Always include: who benefits, who loses, and by how much.
