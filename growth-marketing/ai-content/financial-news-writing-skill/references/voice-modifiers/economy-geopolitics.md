# Voice Modifier: Economy > Geopolitics

## Required Data Points
- Countries/parties involved
- Specific event or escalation
- Commodity price impact (oil, gold)
- Market/currency impact
- Historical comparison (one sentence)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Focus on market-relevant implications, not geopolitical analysis.
"The Strait of Hormuz handles 21% of global oil trade" is essential context.

## Tone Calibration
- Stick to market impact. This is a markets desk, not a foreign-policy journal.
- Quantify the risk premium: options skew, CDS widening, defense sector moves.
- Historical analog: "the last time [similar event], [asset] moved [X%]."
