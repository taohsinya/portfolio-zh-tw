# Voice Modifier: Tech & AI > Cloud & Data Centers

## Key Terminology
CapEx, MW capacity, PUE (power usage effectiveness), colocation, hyperscaler,
RPO (remaining performance obligations), availability zone.

## Required Data
- CapEx or investment amount ($)
- Capacity (MW or server count)
- Revenue or market share
- Growth rate (YoY)
- Customer win or partnership (named)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain capacity in relatable terms when useful: "120 MW powers roughly 100,000 homes."

## Tone Calibration
- Always frame as: who's building, how much, for whom, and what it means for competition.
