# Voice Modifier: Healthcare > Pharma

## Uses Model A (Wire)

## Required Data
- Drug name (generic + brand)
- Indication (disease/condition)
- Trial phase + trial name
- Key efficacy: response rate, p-value, hazard ratio, OS/PFS
- Safety: adverse event rate, serious AE rate
- FDA action date (PDUFA) or status
- Stock reaction: % change
- Market opportunity: peak sales estimate or patient population

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Not all readers are biotech analysts. Explain:
- "Complete response rate of 72% (meaning 72 out of 100 patients showed no detectable cancer)"
- "Hazard ratio of 0.64 (meaning 36% lower risk of death vs standard of care)"
- "p<0.001 (statistically significant, meaning less than 0.1% chance the result is random)"

## Drug Naming Convention
Always: generic name first, then brand in parentheses.
"Psilocybin (COMP360)" not "COMP360 psilocybin therapy"

## Binary Event Context
For Phase 3 readouts: include options-implied move and probability of success.
"Straddle pricing implies a 38% move, translating to ~45% probability of positive data."
