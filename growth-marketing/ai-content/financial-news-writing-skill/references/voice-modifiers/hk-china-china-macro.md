# Voice Modifier: HK & China > China Macro

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Data release or policy decision name (PMI, LPR, CPI, GDP, social financing, etc.)
- Actual value + consensus estimate + prior period
- PBoC action: rate level + change + tool used (MLF, OMO, RRR, LPR)
- Cross-asset impact: at least 2 of — CNY/CNH, A-shares (CSI 300), HSI, China govt bond yield, iron ore/copper
- Historical comparison (one sentence)
- Next decision date or data release date

## China Macro-Specific Rules
- Central bank: "People's Bank of China" (PBoC) on first mention, "PBoC" thereafter
- Currency: distinguish onshore yuan (CNY) and offshore yuan (CNH). "USD/CNH" for offshore, "USD/CNY" for onshore.
- Policy tools: name the specific instrument — "1-year MLF rate" not just "rates"
- Data sources: NBS (National Bureau of Statistics), PBoC, Caixin/S&P Global (for Caixin PMI vs official PMI)
- A-shares: reference CSI 300 or Shanghai Composite with exact level
- Property: include property investment, new home prices (70-city index), or land sales if relevant
- Social financing: aggregate social financing (TSF) is a key credit metric — include if available

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
China macro stories need more context than US/EU macro because the policy transmission mechanism is different (state-directed credit, window guidance, etc.). Explain the "so what" for global investors explicitly.
