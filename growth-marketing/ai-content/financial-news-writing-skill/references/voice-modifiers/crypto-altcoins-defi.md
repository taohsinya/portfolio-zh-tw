# Voice Modifier: Crypto > Altcoins & DeFi

## Always Include
- Token name + ticker + chain: "UNI on Ethereum"
- Price (precision per Model D rules)
- 24h change %
- TVL or protocol revenue (for DeFi) — source: DefiLlama
- Key on-chain metric (active addresses, tx volume)
- Catalyst event (named)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Not all readers follow every DeFi protocol. One sentence explaining what the protocol does.
"Ondo Finance tokenizes short-term US Treasuries, offering 4.6% yield on-chain."

## Token Unlock Warning
If token unlock: exact amount, % of circulating supply, date, vesting schedule source.
