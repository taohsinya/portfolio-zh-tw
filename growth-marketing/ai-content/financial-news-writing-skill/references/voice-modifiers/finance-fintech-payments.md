# Voice Modifier: Finance > Fintech & Payments

## Uses Model C (Industry)

## Required Data
- Company/product name
- TPV (total payment volume) or transaction count
- Revenue or take rate
- User/merchant count
- Growth rate (YoY)
- Competitive positioning

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain take rate: "Stripe's take rate of 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction means it earns $3.20 on a $100 purchase."

## Unit Economics Focus
Fintech stories live or die on unit economics. Always include:
CAC (customer acquisition cost), LTV, payback period if available.
