# Voice Modifier: Crypto > Bitcoin & Macro

## Always Include
- BTC price (exact to $1) + 24h change (% and $)
- Volume (24h, vs 7d avg)
- Market cap + dominance %
- Key support AND resistance levels
- OI + funding rate (exact, e.g., "+0.012%")
- Timestamp with timezone

## Explanation Depth: LOW
BTC audience is sophisticated. No need to explain what Bitcoin is.
Focus on: price action → flow data → macro catalyst → next level.

## Halving Context
When relevant, include: days since halving, where in the cycle, historical pattern.
