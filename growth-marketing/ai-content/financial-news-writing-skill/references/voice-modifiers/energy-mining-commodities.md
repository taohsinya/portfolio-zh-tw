# Voice Modifier: Energy > Mining & Commodities

## Uses Model D (On-chain/Technical)

## Required Data
- Commodity name + spot price + change
- Key inventory/stockpile data + source
- Supply disruption or expansion (named mine/project)
- Demand driver (named sector/application)

## Naming Convention
Always name the benchmark/exchange:
- "LME copper" not "copper"
- "COMEX gold" not "gold"
- "WTI crude" not "oil"
- "iron ore (SGX 62% Fe fines)" not "iron ore"

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Include supply/demand balance: "inventory draws of 15,000 tonnes vs 5-year average build of 8,000."
