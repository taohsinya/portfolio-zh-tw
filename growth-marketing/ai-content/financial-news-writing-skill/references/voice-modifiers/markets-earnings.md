# Voice Modifier: Markets > Earnings

## Sector-Specific Vocabulary
Adapt the key operating metric by sector:
| Sector | Lead With | Terminology |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Semiconductor | Data center revenue %, HBM shipments | wafer starts, ASP, design win |
| Cloud/SaaS | ARR, NRR, RPO | net retention, logo count, upsell |
| Oil & Gas | Production BOE/d, breakeven | realized price, hedging %, AISC |
| Banking | NIM, NII, CET1 | provision, net charge-off, tangible book |
| Retail | Same-store sales, inventory days | comparable, ticket size, footfall |
| Pharma | Pipeline milestone, peak sales est | PDUFA, NDA, sNDA, sBLA |
| EV | Deliveries, ASP, gross margin/vehicle | take rate, ASP erosion |

## Beat/Miss Table (mandatory for all earnings articles)
```
| Metric | Actual | Consensus | Beat/Miss |
|--------|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Revenue | $X.XB | $X.XB | +X.X% |
| EPS | $X.XX | $X.XX | +$X.XX |
| [Sector KPI] | X | X | +/-X% |
```

## Explanation Depth: LOW
Reader knows what EPS and revenue mean. Do NOT explain basic financial terms.
Focus on: what beat, what missed, what changed in guidance, and why.

## Tone Calibration
- Zero editorializing. Let numbers tell the story.
- Urgency: write as if the trader is reading this in the 30 seconds after the release.
