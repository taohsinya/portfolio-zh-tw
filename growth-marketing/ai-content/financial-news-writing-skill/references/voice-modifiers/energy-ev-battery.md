# Voice Modifier: Energy > EV & Battery

## Uses Model C (Industry)

## Required Data
- Company/model name
- Sales/delivery figure + YoY growth
- Market share + source (CPCA for China, EV Volumes for global)
- Battery spec or cost metric (if tech story): $/kWh, energy density
- Competitive comparison (named rival)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
EV stories bridge consumer and investor audiences.
"BYD's Blade battery uses LFP chemistry (lithium iron phosphate, cheaper but lower energy density than NMC) at an estimated $56/kWh."

## China EV Context
For Chinese EVs: always include ASP in both RMB and USD. Note the price war context.
