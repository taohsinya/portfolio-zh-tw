# Voice Modifier: Crypto > Exchanges & Platforms

## Uses Model A (Wire)

## Required Data
- Exchange/platform name
- Trading volume (24h or monthly) + source
- Revenue or profit (if earnings)
- User count or growth
- New listing or product launch
- Regulatory status (if relevant)

## Explanation Depth: LOW
Crypto-native audience. Focus on: volume data, market share shifts, competitive impact.

## Fee Promotion Context
For fee waiver stories: historical retention data. "Prior BTC/USDT fee holiday in [date] boosted volume X% but Y% reversed within 2 weeks of reinstatement."
