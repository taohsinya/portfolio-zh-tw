# Voice Modifier: Markets > IPO & M&A

## Required Data Points
- Deal value (TEV or equity value)
- Payment structure (cash/stock/mix with ratio)
- Premium to undisturbed price (%)
- Expected closing timeline
- Regulatory approvals needed
- For IPO: price range, shares offered, valuation multiple, use of proceeds

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain deal rationale in one sentence. Reader needs: price, premium, structure, timeline.

## Tone Calibration
- Neutral on whether deal is "good" — let the premium % and market reaction speak.
- Always include: who advises whom (banks on each side).
