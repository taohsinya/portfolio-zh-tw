# Voice Modifier: Crypto > Stablecoins & CBDC

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Stablecoin name(s) + issuer(s)
- Market cap or supply (source: DefiLlama)
- Peg status (on/off, deviation)
- Regulatory action or policy
- Affected jurisdiction(s)

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Stablecoin regulation stories reach a broad audience including TradFi readers. Explain:
- What a stablecoin is (one sentence, for new readers)
- How the regulatory framework differs from banking
- The reserve/backing structure
