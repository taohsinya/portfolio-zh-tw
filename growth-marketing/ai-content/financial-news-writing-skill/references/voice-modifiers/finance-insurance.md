# Voice Modifier: Finance > Insurance

## Uses Model A (Wire)

## Required Data
- Company + ticker
- Combined ratio (below 100% = profitable underwriting)
- Written premium growth (%)
- Loss ratio
- Investment income
- Reserve development (favorable/adverse)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain combined ratio: "A combined ratio of 92% means the insurer earns $8 in underwriting profit for every $100 in premiums."

## Catastrophe Context
For P&C insurers: always note cat losses separately from attritional losses.
"Excluding $420M in hurricane losses, the underlying combined ratio was 88.2%."
