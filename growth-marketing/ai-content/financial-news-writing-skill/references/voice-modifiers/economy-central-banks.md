# Voice Modifier: Economy > Central Banks

## Required Data Points
- Institution (Fed, ECB, BoE, BoJ, PBoC)
- Decision: rate level + change
- Vote split if available
- Key quote from statement or press conference
- Forward guidance summary (hawkish/dovish + specific wording)
- Market reaction: at least 2 assets (yield + equity or FX)
- Next meeting date

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Explain the policy transmission mechanism. Not all readers follow central bank jargon.
Translate "dot plot" → "individual rate projections from 19 Fed officials."
Translate "hawkish hold" → "held rates but signaled fewer future cuts."

## Tone Calibration
- Forward guidance IS the story. Quote it verbatim, then interpret.
- Always include the futures market pricing (CME FedWatch or equivalent).
- Historical comparison: "the last time the Fed used this language was [date], which preceded [outcome]."
