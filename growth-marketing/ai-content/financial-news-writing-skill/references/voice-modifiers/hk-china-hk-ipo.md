# Voice Modifier: HK & China > HK IPO

## Uses Model A (Wire)

## Required Data
- Company name (English + Chinese) + proposed ticker
- Offer price or indicative range (HKD)
- Deal size (HK$ or US$)
- Oversubscription ratio (retail + institutional separately)
- Cornerstone investors: names + committed amounts
- Use of proceeds breakdown (% allocation)
- Listing date
- Lead underwriter(s)
- Valuation metric (P/E, P/S, EV/EBITDA) vs comparable listed peers

## HK IPO-Specific Rules
- Always state listing board: "Main Board" or "GEM"
- Clawback mechanism: note if triggered and at what level
- Greenshoe option: size if disclosed
- Lockup period for cornerstone investors
- Currency: HKD for offer price, convert to USD for deal size context
- Regulatory: SFC approval status if pending

## Explanation Depth: LOW
Pure data — this is a wire story. Get the numbers out fast. One sentence of business context max.
