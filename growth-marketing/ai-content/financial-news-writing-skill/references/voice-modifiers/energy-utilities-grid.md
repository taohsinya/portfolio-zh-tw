# Voice Modifier: Energy > Utilities & Grid

## Uses Model A (Wire)

## Required Data
- Utility company name + ticker
- Rate change or capex figure
- Demand growth (% or MW)
- Earnings/revenue (if earnings story)
- Regulatory proceeding status

## Explanation Depth: LOW
Utility investors know rate cases and demand growth. Focus on: numbers, timeline, regulatory outcome.

## Key Context
Data center electricity demand is the current macro driver. Always note if a utility's growth is driven by hyperscaler contracts.
