# Voice Modifier: Markets > Shareholder Actions

## Story Types & Lead Data
| Action | Lead With |
|--------|-----------|
| Buyback | Dollar amount + % of market cap + timeframe |
| Dividend | New rate + yield + vs prior rate |
| Activist stake | Stake size + % ownership + demand |
| Insider trade | Name + title + shares + $ amount + Form 4 date |
| Stock split | Ratio + effective date + new price |

## Explanation Depth: LOW
Reader knows what a buyback or 13D filing is. Focus on: magnitude, motivation, market reaction.

## Tone Calibration
- For activist stakes: present the activist's thesis and management's likely response neutrally.
- For insider trades: context matters — is this routine (10b5-1 plan) or discretionary?
