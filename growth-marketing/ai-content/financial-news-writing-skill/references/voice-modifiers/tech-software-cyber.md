# Voice Modifier: Tech & AI > Software & Cyber

## Key Terminology
ARR, NRR (net revenue retention), RPO, logo count, DBNR, TAM, freemium, PLG.

## Required Data
- ARR or revenue figure
- NRR (>120% is strong, <100% is red flag)
- Customer count or growth
- Competitive positioning (market share or ranking)

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Software investors know SaaS metrics. Focus on what's changing: "NRR fell from 128% to 115%, the first decline in 8 quarters."

## Tone Calibration
- Cybersecurity: emphasize breach context only when directly relevant. Don't sensationalize.
- For IPO-stage companies: ARR growth + burn rate + runway = the story.
