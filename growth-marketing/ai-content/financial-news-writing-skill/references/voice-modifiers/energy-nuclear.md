# Voice Modifier: Energy > Nuclear

## Uses Model C (Industry)

## Required Data
- Reactor type (SMR, Gen IV, conventional)
- Technology name + developer
- Capacity (MWe or MWth)
- Fuel type (LEU, HALEU — explain enrichment level)
- Regulatory status (NRC license, COL application)
- Policy driver (IRA, DOE funding program)

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Nuclear is technically dense and politically charged. Explain clearly:
- "HALEU (uranium enriched to 19.75%, vs 3-5% for conventional reactors)"
- "SMR (small modular reactor, typically <300 MWe, factory-built)"

## Tone Calibration
- Technical neutrality. No advocacy for or against nuclear.
- Always include: timeline to commercial operation, not just announcement.
