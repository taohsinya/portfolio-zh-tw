# Voice Modifier: Crypto > Regulation & Policy

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Regulatory body + jurisdiction
- Specific rule or enforcement action
- Affected tokens/exchanges/protocols
- Fine amount or penalty (if enforcement)
- Implementation timeline

## Explanation Depth: HIGH
Regulation stories must be precise about what IS regulated vs what ISN'T.
Quote the specific statute or rule. Name the agency and the official who announced it.

## Tone Calibration
- Neutral on whether regulation is "good" or "bad."
- Always include industry reaction from a named exchange or trade group.
