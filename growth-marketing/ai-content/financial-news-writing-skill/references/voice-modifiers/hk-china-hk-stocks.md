# Voice Modifier: HK & China > HK Stocks

## Uses Model E (Equity Market)

## Required Data
- HSI and/or HSTECH index level + exact point/% change
- Session high and low
- Turnover vs 20-day average (HK$ billion)
- Stock Connect net flow direction + amount (northbound/southbound)
- Top 3 gaining and losing sectors with % moves
- Primary catalyst (named event, NOT "sentiment")
- At least 1 cross-asset reference (CNH/CNY, US futures, A-shares)

## HK-Specific Rules
- Currency: HKD for HK-listed, RMB for dual-counter. Always specify.
- Index names: "Hang Seng Index" (HSI), "Hang Seng Tech Index" (HSTECH), never abbreviate without first use.
- Trading hours: reference HKT (UTC+8). Morning session 09:30-12:00, afternoon 13:00-16:00.
- Stock Connect: always specify direction — "southbound" (mainland → HK) or "northbound" (HK → mainland) with net buy/sell amount in HK$ or RMB.
- Company names: English name first, Chinese in parentheses on first mention. E.g., "Tencent Holdings (騰訊控股, 0700.HK)"
- Ticker format: "0700.HK" or "9988.HK" — always include .HK suffix.

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Focus on what drove the session. Connect to mainland A-share moves, US overnight session, and policy catalysts.
