# Voice Modifier: Energy > Clean Energy

## Uses Model C (Industry)

## Required Data
- Project name + location
- Capacity (MW or GW)
- Investment amount ($)
- Timeline (construction start, completion)
- Policy driver (IRA Section 45V, EU Green Deal, etc.)
- LCOE or cost metric if available

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
Explain policy incentives: "IRA Section 45V provides up to $3/kg in tax credits for green hydrogen."

## Tone Calibration
- Frame around economics, not advocacy. "Wind LCOE fell to $32/MWh" matters more than "clean energy is the future."
