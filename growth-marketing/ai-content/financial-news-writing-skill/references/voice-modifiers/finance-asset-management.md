# Voice Modifier: Finance > Asset Management

## Uses Model B (Macro)

## Required Data
- Firm name
- AUM figure
- Fund inflows/outflows (net, period)
- Strategy type
- Performance metric (IRR, return vs benchmark)
- Fee rate if relevant

## Explanation Depth: MEDIUM
- "AUM of $3.8T" needs context: "up 8% from end of 2024, driven by $X in net inflows and $Y in market appreciation."
- Distinguish between fundraising (new commitments) and deployment (invested capital).

## PE/VC Specific
For PE: hold period, DPI (distributions to paid-in), TVPI, unrealized value.
For VC: vintages, markdowns, follow-on reserve ratios.
