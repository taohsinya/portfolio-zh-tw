# Wire Template (Model A)

Use for: Markets>Earnings, Markets>Analyst Moves, Healthcare>Pharma, Finance>Insurance

---

## Output Format

```markdown
# [Title: ≤11 words, sentence case, lead with ticker or company name]

[LEDE: One sentence, ≤25 words. Lead with the NUMBER, not the company.
Format: "[Company] [reported/posted] [key metric] of [value], [beating/missing] estimates by [amount]."
Example: "Micron reported Q2 revenue of $8.71B, beating consensus by 12%, driven by record HBM sales."]

**Key Takeaways:**
- [What happened: key metric + vs consensus + vs prior period]
- [Why it matters: which segment drove it, what changed]
- [Market reaction: stock move + after-hours if applicable]

## [H2 #1: What Happened — headline includes the key number]

[200-300 words. Walk through the results chronologically or by importance.
Every number needs: value + unit + context (vs consensus, vs prior quarter, YoY).
Name the CEO or CFO if they provided guidance.
Include the guidance range if given.]

## [H2 #2: Market Reaction — headline includes the stock move]

[100-200 words. Stock price reaction with % and direction.
After-hours move if different from regular session.
Analyst reactions: name the firm, name the analyst, give their rating + PT change.
Peer/sector impact if significant.]

---
*Disclaimer: This article is for informational purposes only and does not constitute investment advice. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Always conduct your own research before making investment decisions.*
```

## Checklist Before Output

- [ ] Lede ≤ 25 words
- [ ] Title ≤ 11 words, sentence case
- [ ] Every number has value + unit + context
- [ ] At least 1 named analyst or source
- [ ] Guidance included (if earnings)
- [ ] Stock reaction included
- [ ] Total word count: 400-600
- [ ] Zero banned words (run against banned-words.md)
