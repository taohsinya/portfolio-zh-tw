# Macro Analysis Template (Model B)

Use for: Economy (all), Energy>Oil, Finance>Banking, Finance>Real Estate

---

## Output Format

```markdown
# [Title: ≤11 words, sentence case, lead with the causal insight not the event]

[LEDE: One sentence, ≤35 words. Lead with the INSIGHT, not the data release.
Format: "[Data point] signals [implication], [connecting to broader trend]."
Bad: "The Bureau of Labor Statistics released March CPI data today."
Good: "US core CPI fell to 3.1%, its lowest since March 2021, giving the Fed room to cut rates as early as June."]

**Key Takeaways:**
- [What happened: data point + value + vs expectation + vs prior]
- [Why it matters: cause-effect chain, what this changes for markets/policy]
- [What's next: next decision date, data release, or inflection point to watch]

## [H2 #1: What Happened — headline includes the data point and direction]

[200-300 words. Report the data with full context.
Headline number vs consensus vs prior period.
Break down components (e.g., for CPI: shelter, food, energy, core services).
Historical context: where does this sit in the cycle?
Name the source agency and release time.]

## [H2 #2: Why It Matters — headline names the causal mechanism]

[200-350 words. This is the analysis section — the VALUE of Model B.
Connect the data to policy (Fed, ECB, BOJ, PBOC).
Connect to market pricing (futures, rate expectations, yield curve).
Cross-asset implications (bonds → equities → FX → commodities).
Name at least one economist or strategist with a direct quote or view.]

## [H2 #3: What's Next — headline names the date or event]

[100-200 words. Forward calendar: next data release, next meeting, next decision.
Scenario framing: "If [X], then [Y]. If [not X], then [Z]."
One key risk that could invalidate the current trajectory.]

---
*Disclaimer: This article is for informational purposes only and does not constitute investment advice. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Always conduct your own research before making investment decisions.*
```

## Checklist Before Output

- [ ] Lede ≤ 35 words, leads with insight
- [ ] Title ≤ 11 words, sentence case
- [ ] Cause-effect chain is explicit (not implied)
- [ ] At least 1 named economist/strategist
- [ ] Cross-asset connection present
- [ ] Forward calendar with specific date
- [ ] No hollow macro words without numbers (check banned-words.md macro section)
- [ ] Total word count: 600-900
- [ ] Zero banned words
